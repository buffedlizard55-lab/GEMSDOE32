IR-32-BAR-02mediumThe 'adaptive credit bar' that this project has repeatedly cited as its stopping rule CANNOT BIND on an emission confined to its own belief support. For a support pixel of a binary field, kbar(x) = sum_delta field(x+delta)k(delta) >= field(x)k(0) = 1, so the added false-positive mass 1 - kbar <= 0 and the acceptance inequality holds unconditionally for any s < 1/alpha = 5. Measured: emitter.emit(field, budget=None, prior_dti=0.26) consumes the ENTIRE 121,131-px H19-5 support and terminates with stopped_by='exhausted_support'.Stated openly in knowledge/02 section 4b. The bar is still the correct rule for deciding whether a dot PAYS; it is simply not a constraint on an emission that never leaves its own support. Budget must therefore come from somewhere else -- which is exactly why the three-slot identification experiment that measures the hidden-set quantities is the next action rather than another budget sweep.
