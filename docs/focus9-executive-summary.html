3. Why the best-scoring file is not the best predictor by the repo's own gate (irregularity IR-20261029-A)

On the nine owner-reported public scores, the two local holdouts disagree:

  • SGMC-CAL (off-catalogue, prevalence-calibrated): Spearman ρ = +0.950 with the public scores (n = 9, p = 9e-05). This is the instrument that matches the competition's stated target: faults not in the USGS catalogue.
  • HIDE-RECOVER (catalogue-withheld, the repo's current gate): Spearman ρ = -0.849. Its ranking is anti-aligned with the public one.

The 0.2778 champion scores 0.0045 on HIDE-RECOVER (near the bottom of the nine) and 0.0641 on SGMC-CAL (second of nine). The repo's gate asks for a HIDE-RECOVER win, so it rejects the files that the off-catalogue evidence ranks highest. Changing the gate instrument is an owner decision; this summary does not change it.

Caveats: n = 9 owner-reported labels, and several of them are the same dot set, so the effective sample size is much smaller. Treat these correlations as a finding to test, not a result.
