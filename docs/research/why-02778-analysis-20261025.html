1. The metric is a budget, not a count

Official DTI: k(d)=max(1-d/R,0), TP_w = sum_g max_x p(x)k(d), DTI = TP_w/(TP_w+0.2FP_w+0.8FN_w) = T/(0.2(T+S-M)+0.8|G|).
