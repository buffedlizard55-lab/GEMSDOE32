## 1. Why `h33-h33-2-b2` (`0.2778` `OWNER-REPORT`) Leads the 9 Focus Sites & Why Single-Regime Detectors Hit `IR-32-SARPU-PARETO-01`

### 1.1 Exact Metric Identity & Byte-Verified Pruning Ladder
Under the official DOE GEMS Distance-Weighted Tversky Index (`DTI`, [DrivenData Problem Page 967](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/)) with $\alpha = 0.2$, $\beta = 0.8$, 300 m (`R = 3 px` at `100 m` grid resolution in `EPSG:32611`) triangular kernel $K(u) = \max(0, 1 - u/3)$, and known-catalogue fault pixels (`existing_faults.tif`, `60,988` positive cells inside the `5,167,373`-cell active footprint) masked before scoring ([Staff Forum Clarification #11516](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516)):

$$\text{DTI}(P, G) = \frac{T}{T + 0.2\,FP + 0.8\,FN} = \frac{T}{0.2(T + S - M) + 0.8|G|},$$

where $S = \sum_x p(x)$ is total emitted prediction mass, $T = \sum_{g \in G} \max_x [p(x) K(d(x, g))]$ is weighted true-positive coverage of hidden truth $G$, and $M = \sum_x p(x)\max_{g \in G} K(d(x, g))$.

Byte-level SHA-256 inspection across the 9 focus sites establishes an exact nested subset hierarchy (where pixel counts and zero-XOR inclusions are verified byte facts, while historical leaderboard numbers are `OWNER-REPORT` without cryptographic organizer receipts in this repository):
1. `H19-5` (`19GEMSDOE`, `121,131` px; `0.1922 OWNER-REPORT`) $\supset$ `D1.5` (`GEMSDOE24`, `60,069` px; `0.2477 OWNER-REPORT`) $\supset$ `D2.8` (`GEMSDOE25` / `GEMSDOE30`, zero in-footprint XOR, `44,090` px; `0.2600 OWNER-REPORT`): thinning multi-pixel ribbons at kernel spacing `d = 2.8 px` eliminates `-63.6%` redundant flank/along-strike prediction mass $S$ while preserving 300 m triangular-kernel coverage $T$.
2. `D2.8` (`44,090` px) $\supset$ `B=1` (`GEMSDOE28` / `GEMSDOE31` solo, `40,199` px = `D2.8` minus `3,891` pixels at $0 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 1\text{ px}$; `0.2708 OWNER-REPORT`) $\supset$ `B=2` (`GEMSDOE32` Champion `h33-h33-2-b2`, `37,654` px = `D2.8` minus `6,436` pixels at $0 < d_{\text{cat}} \le 2\text{ px}$; `0.2778 OWNER-REPORT`). Removing predictions in the `0–200 m` digitizing/erosional scarp-retreat apron of already-mapped faults cuts near-catalogue false positives. Conversely, `GEMSDOE36` (`38,854` px = exact `B=2` + `1,200` extra dots; `0.2750 OWNER-REPORT`) demonstrates that adding dots below the marginal DTI credit bar lowers the score.
