## 1. Two Line-by-Line Primary-Data Irregularities Discovered Prior to Implementation

Before designing new hypotheses, direct byte-level inspection of the restored competition and USGS rasters (`data/restore_receipt.json`, 23/23 SHA-256 verified) uncovered two material defects affecting prior `GEMSDOE32` feature modules:

1. **`IR-32-BAND6-TC-01` — Band 6 (`tc`) in `training_features.tif` is GeoDAWN Radiometric Total Count, NOT Magnetic Tilt Angle:**
   - The GeoTIFF metadata tag on Band 6 reads `data_category='magnetic_data'`, `description='Tilt angle or total curvature - magnetic field derivative for edge detection'`.
   - Direct pixel-wise verification across all `5,164,300` valid footprint pixels proves Band 6 (`tc`, range `2.08–88.3`, strictly positive) correlates at **`r = 0.997140`** with USGS GeoDAWN Airborne Gamma-Ray Radiometric Total Count (`external/geodawn_rad_u8.tif` Band 4 `TC`, [DOI 10.5066/P93LGLVQ](https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ)), and at **`r = 0.012796`** with the true Miller & Singh (1994) magnetic tilt angle $\theta_{\text{TMI}} = \arctan(\text{tmi\_vg} / |\text{tmi\_hg}|)$ computed from Bands 9 and 3.
   - Consequence: `Arena41` (`src/gems32/pfonly.py`) inadvertently included surface gamma-ray radiometrics in its "potential-field-only" model, while `H35-B` (`src/gems32/h35_new.py`) treated `tc = 0` as a magnetic contact zero-crossing. True magnetic tilt-angle zero-crossings and Salem et al. (2007) tilt-depth have never been computed in `GEMSDOE32`.

2. **`IR-32-LIDAR-BANDS-01` — Mis-indexed 1 m LiDAR Scarp Channels and Unmasked Missing-Tile Zeros (`24.63%` of Footprint):**
   - `external/lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif` contains **12 bands**: `1=ex_max`, `2=ex_mean`, `3=step_max`, `4=lapneg_max`, `5=lappos_max`, `6=downface_max`, `7=upface_max`, `8=cross_max`, `9=relief`, `10=coh100`, `11=strike`, `12=valid`.
   - Prior modules (`src/gems32/h33.py`, `src/gems32/hypotheses.py`, `src/gems32/h35_new.py`) read bands `(1, 2, 5)` while claiming in docstrings to read `(ex_max, step_max, downface_max)` = `(1, 3, 6)`, never read one-sided scarp polarity (`|downface_max - upface_max|`), cross-strike tectonic asymmetry (`cross_max`), 100 m scarp coherence (`coh100`), or `strike` (`Band 11`), and treated the **`1,272,724` footprint pixels (`24.63%`) where `valid == 0` (no 1 m 3DEP LiDAR tile flown)** as zero scarp evidence — penalizing non-LiDAR tiles by up to `55%`.

3. **`IR-32-INSTR-03` — Shared Holdout Evaluator Fix Required by Protocol Rule #2:**
   - `src/gems32/holdout.py` scored `B=2`-gated rasters backwards (`0.00460 < 0.03104 < 0.09832` vs live `0.2778 > 0.2708 > 0.2600`) when callers masked `d_all_labels <= 2` using withheld hidden test segments. Per Protocol Rule #2 (*"derive every catalogue-based feature only from the visible faults, mask visible faults pixel-exactly, score pooled DTI ... If a shared tool is wrong, fix it once in the template and report it"*), `src/gems32/holdout.py` is upgraded (`GEMSDOE32-HOLDOUT-v3-visible-only`) to store per-cell `visible_sub` and `d_visible_sub` and evaluate visible-only `B`-collars cleanly.

## 3. Single-Lane Experimental Plan (`A8A-H1: MDRC`, Budget $\le 3$ Experiments)

- **Experiment 1 (`EXP1` — Leakage Canary + Solo Physical Domain Screen):**
  1. Build/reuse the 35-channel template stack (`.cache/gems_work/feature_stack.npy`) and compute 22 label-free `MDRC` channels across Domains I–IV.
  2. Run direction-free ROC-AUC canary ($\max(\text{AUC}, 1-\text{AUC})$) on **every single feature channel** (pooled and per-quadrant). Any channel $> 0.90$ halts execution immediately.
  3. Screen each unsupervised domain surface alone (`D1_LiDAR_Dipole`, `D2_True_Mag_Tilt`, `D3_Grav_MT_Step`, `D4_Hydrothermal_Alt`, and label-free `MDRC_Unsup_4Domain`) on repaired visible-only `HOLDOUT-DTI` (`GEMSDOE32-HOLDOUT-v3-visible-only`, 8 quadrant-draws, 22,232 withheld positives) and off-catalogue `SGMC` at matched 37,654 dots.
- **Experiment 2 (`EXP2` — Spatially-Blocked 4-Quadrant Visible-Only OOF `MDRC` vs `CTRL-35ch`):**
  1. In every holdout cell/quadrant (15-px = 1.5 km collar), fit strictly on **visible** catalogue faults outside the test quadrant + collar (`0` withheld positives seen in training, `0` SGMC faults used in training).
  2. Compare `CTRL-35ch` (template 35-ch `HistGB`) vs `MDRC-DualOOF` (57-ch general + piedmont/cover-propensity `HistGB` ensemble $\times$ smooth multi-domain corroboration gate), emitted via visible-only `B=2` collar + exact `gems32.emitter.emit` at `37,654` dots.
- **Experiment 3 (`EXP3` — Oriented 1-px Cross-Strike Crest Sharpening & Credit-Bar Operating Point):**
  1. Test 1-px oriented cross-strike crest sharpening on `MDRC-DualOOF` (`MDRC-Crest-37654` vs unsharpened `MDRC-DualOOF-37654` vs analytic marginal credit-bar stopping at $\tau = 0.2 \times 0.2778 = 0.05556$).
  2. Select the best pre-registered `MDRC` arm on visible-only `HOLDOUT-DTI`, run pre-placement surface uniqueness and post-placement dot uniqueness against all registry rasters, and emit the unique GeoTIFF deliverable via `gems32.submission.write_submission_pair` + independent `scripts/check_submission.py`.
