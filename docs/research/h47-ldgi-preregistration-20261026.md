1. **US Census TIGER/Line 2024 Highway & Railroad Cut-and-Fill Embankments:** Linear engineered road/railroad prisms produce sharp 1 m LiDAR scarps and cultural magnetic noise; suppressed via `tiger_road_clean_gate` ($1 - \exp(-(d_{\text{road}}/150\text{ m})^2)$) across all topographic and wavelet channels.
