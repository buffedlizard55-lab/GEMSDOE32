IR-PORTAL-01: DrivenData Web Validator Range Rejection on NaNs

Engineered & Resolved

Observation: DrivenData web portal returns "Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]" when GeoTIFF has NaN outside the data footprint.
Mitigation: Set unpredicted outside pixels to finite 0.0 (float32). Local and portal validation passes cleanly with zero errors.

IR-25-COMPARATOR-DRIFT: Local Full-Catalogue vs Hidden Discovery Inversion

Calibrated

Observation: Local full-catalogue evaluation ranks unthinned lines higher because all training labels are present. On the real hidden test set, known training labels are masked out, so unthinned lines suffer huge FP penalties.
Mitigation: Strict spatial block holdout cross-validation with catalogue positive masking.
